# Rally One — Tonight's 3-minute Dustin & Mandy demonstration

**Objective:** Show *a church companion that responds to a real next action*, not five disconnected polished screens. The interactive demo is fictional, browser-local and uses user-supplied visual portraits for design only. Do not imply any sample message, attendance, prayer or photo identifies an authenticated church account.

## Before you see them (two-minute preflight)
1. Open the **showcase comparison Netlify deploy-preview**, not production. In iPhone Safari, use a clean tab or private window to avoid old saved fixture state. Verify the app loads and the official Citizen flag and approved charcoal/Rally One branding are intact.
2. If prompted, choose **Log in → Demo**, then tap the **round profile control** in the top-right and verify **Leadership demo · One-tap journeys** shows five scenarios. It should appear at the top of the modal.
3. Tap **05 · Group + Serving** and confirm Today's red priority is **Your Wednesday Group** with an **RSVP** action and a second serving response. Verify the red card fits full width; the people circles below Today match Groups exactly.
4. Check one group profile, then return to Today. Close/reopen the demo controls: selecting the same scenario returns RSVP/shift to pending, **without deleting Connect messages or Scripture progress**.
5. If *anything* feels off, fall back immediately to the proven Sprint 7 preview at https://deploy-preview-113--citizen-connect-test.netlify.app. No production app is affected.

## The 3-minute story (rehearse once)
**00:00–00:20 — The hook.** Show the Rally One opening/Today. Say: *“We asked what happens after people walk through the doors. How do we help them actually belong and be cared for? This is a working prototype of one answer.”*

**00:20–01:00 — Real action, real change.** With **05 · Group + Serving**, point to Today's one main RSVP action and one supporting serving action. Tap **RSVP · I’m in**. Watch the primary recommendation change. Say: *“It doesn't just remind you—it knows when you've acted and what matters next. It doesn't send anything without you.”* Tap **Groups** and confirm the Wednesday gathering's RSVP shows Going. Return.

**01:00–01:35 — People first.** Scroll to the same Instagram-style portrait circle rail on Today or Groups. Tap a supplied Dustin or Mandy photo to reveal the fictional example profile, then return. Say: *“This can be a meaningful connection back to real people, not a weekly email turned into app tiles.”* Clearly explain that photos are illustrative; authentication and people relationships are not connected.

**01:35–02:10 — Follow-through.** If Today shows an unread group conversation, open it through **Connect**; inspect the discussion, reply/reaction tools and return. This is only a local demo thread. The real demo read cursor—not an invented unread count—determines what Today displays next. Alternatively open **Team**, confirm a sample serving response and show Today and Team agree.

**02:10–02:40 — Purposeful personalization.** Open the top-right profile control again. Switch to **01 · First-time visitor**. Today becomes a gentle invitation with no private group data. Switch to **04 · Leader fixture**. Leader care is available only in this explicit fictional demo role; no automatic spiritual scoring or outreach.

**02:40–03:00 — The close.** Tap **Ask Rally** on Today to show the existing explainable, context-based companion and the next action, then close: *“Imagine the church having an intentional companion that helps everyone take their next step, while giving leaders better opportunities to personally care for people.”* Acknowledge it's an engineering/UX prototype, not a connected church member system.

## What makes the showcase polish different
The separate comparison adds one-time 300–460ms entrances to the real priority hero, secondary action, glance, groups photo rail and cards; slightly deeper matte shadows; crisper press/focus feedback; and a consistent, more deliberate confirmation notice. **There are no artificial typing indicators, looping motion, fake notifications, generated people or fabricated messages.** Reduced-motion users see the same layout without the new animations.

## What NOT to do just before the meeting
- Do **not** merge draft PRs, change production, connect credentials, authorize member data, or publish sensitive/pastoral content.
- Do **not** do a late global CSS redesign, layer another dashboard on Today, or introduce fake AI responses. Your real value is the connection between the screens.
- Do **not** claim sample Dustin/Mandy photos prove live account identity. The imagery is for this private prototype review; broader publication still needs consent.
- Do **not** wait until the meeting to test Safari scrolling, the keyboard and the full-width 390px priority hero.

## The question to ask leadership
*“If this worked with our actual approved member, group and serving systems—and respected consent and pastoral access—would this help us care for people more intentionally? Which one part would matter most to you?”*

**Keep the demonstration short enough for them to ask questions.** A compact working loop is more persuasive than scrolling through a catalog of features.
